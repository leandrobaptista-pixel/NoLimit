Website privacy & measurement

Updated October 19, 2026

Understanding interest in our work

We collect aggregate counts of public page views, gallery categories, website sections, catalog opens, contact-link clicks and consultation form activity. Referral information is reduced to broad groups such as Google, social, email, other or direct/unknown. Full referral URLs and campaign values are not sent to our measurement service.

Our measurement counters do not contain visitor names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, device fingerprints, precise locations or visitor identifiers. They are stored in hourly totals, with no individual browsing history. We do not use advertising cookies or sell these measurement counters.

A single yes/no flag in session storage avoids counting every page as a new visit in the same tab. It is not an identifier and is cleared when that tab session ends. Visit totals are estimates, not a count of identifiable people. Aggregate counters are retained for up to 90 days.

Your choice

You can turn off optional website measurement in this browser. We also honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals for this measurement. Your privacy choice is stored locally on this device.

Consultation requests

If you choose to contact us, we receive the contact details, project information and files you submit so we can respond and arrange your consultation. These private records are separate from anonymous website counters and are available to authorized staff.

Security and authorized accounts

Our hosting providers may process technical network logs needed to deliver and protect the website. Authorized staff account activity, changes and removals may be logged for security and recovery. Public website measurement is not used to identify anonymous visitors or accuse them of security violations.

For questions, use the contact information on our website.
